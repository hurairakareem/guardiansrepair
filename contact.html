Before you call

A few details that help us help you faster

If you're not sure what's wrong, that's fine — just describe what you're noticing. If you have a moment beforehand, this information helps:

  • What the garage door is doing
  • Whether the door opens or closes at all
  • Whether you hear any unusual sounds
  • Whether a spring or cable appears visibly broken
  • Whether the opener is working
  • Whether the door is physically stuck
  • Your city or ZIP code
  • A photo or video of the problem, if you have one
