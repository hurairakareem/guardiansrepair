Emergency

Emergency garage door repair when you need it

A garage door problem can happen at the worst possible time. A broken spring, damaged cable, stuck door, or malfunctioning opener can leave your vehicle trapped inside — or leave your property unsecured. Contact us to describe the problem and confirm emergency service availability.

What counts as an emergency

Common garage door emergencies

Call for emergency garage door service when you're dealing with problems such as:

  • Garage door won't open or close
  • Broken garage door spring
  • Broken or frayed cable
  • Garage door stuck halfway
  • Door off its tracks
  • Bent or damaged track
  • Garage door suddenly becomes very heavy
  • Damaged door panels
  • Garage door opener failure
  • Door won't respond to the remote or wall control
  • Garage door is stuck in an unsafe position
  • Accidental vehicle impact damage
Spring emergencies

Emergency garage door spring problems

A broken spring can make a door extremely difficult or impossible to open. Springs counterbalance the door's weight, so a failed spring can affect the entire lifting system. Signs of a possible broken spring:

  • Door suddenly won't open
  • Door feels unusually heavy
  • A loud bang was heard from the garage
  • Visible break in the spring
  • Door opens only slightly
  • Opener struggles to lift the door
Cable & track emergencies

Cable problems and off-track doors

Cable problems — a broken or disconnected cable can cause the door to become uneven or unstable. Watch for a loose or frayed cable, a cable off the drum, or the door hanging unevenly. Avoid repeatedly operating the door until it's been inspected.

Door off its tracks — can result from impact damage, broken rollers, bent tracks, cable problems, or door imbalance. Forcing an off-track door can cause additional damage to panels, rollers, tracks, and cables.

Opener emergencies

Emergency opener problems

An opener failure can prevent normal access to your garage. This can include the motor not running, a remote or wall control not responding, the door reversing unexpectedly, or the opener running without moving the door.

The opener isn't always the actual source of the problem — a broken spring, cable, track, or other mechanical issue can also stop the opener from operating the door correctly.

Commercial emergencies

Emergency commercial garage door repair

A commercial door failure can interrupt business operations, deliveries, vehicle access, and facility security. We can assist with urgent commercial problems, subject to service availability — see our commercial services page.

Know when to stop

When you should stop using your garage door

Stop operating the door and request professional assistance if:

  • The door is off its tracks
  • A spring is visibly broken
  • A cable is broken or disconnected
  • The door is severely damaged
  • The door is hanging unevenly
  • The door is making sudden loud mechanical noises
  • The door appears unstable
  • The opener is struggling to lift the door
While you wait

What to do until we arrive

Do not attempt to force a damaged garage door open or closed.

  • Keep the area around the door clear of people and vehicles
  • Don't force a stuck or off-track door manually
  • Unplug or disable the opener if a spring or cable has failed
  • Call us and describe exactly what you're seeing
Common questions

What people ask before calling.

What is considered a garage door emergency?

A broken spring or cable, off-track door, severely damaged door, door stuck in a dangerous position, or a failure that prevents you from securing or accessing your property can all call for urgent service.

Can I manually open my garage door during an opener failure?

It depends on the condition of the door. If it has a broken spring, damaged cable, is off its tracks, or feels unusually heavy, don't attempt to force it open.

Can you repair a garage door at night?

Emergency service hours depend on actual availability at the time — contact us to confirm current emergency service hours in your area.

How quickly can an emergency garage door repair be completed?

It depends on the problem, the condition of the door, the parts required, and the service location.

Should I replace or repair my damaged garage door?

It depends on the extent of the damage, the door's age, the condition of its components, and repair history. An inspection can help determine the right option.
