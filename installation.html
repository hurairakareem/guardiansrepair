Garage Door Installation

Professional garage door installation for your home or business

A new garage door can improve the appearance, security, and everyday convenience of your property. Whether you're replacing an aging door or installing one for new construction, choosing the right door and having it installed correctly matters for safe, dependable operation.

Complete installation

More than just the door panels

A garage door is more than the visible panels — a properly installed system includes multiple components that need to work together correctly. Depending on the door and project, installation may involve:

  • Garage door panels
  • Vertical and horizontal tracks
  • Rollers
  • Hinges
  • Springs
  • Cables
  • Brackets
  • Handles and hardware
  • Garage door opener
  • Safety sensors
  • Weather seals and bottom seals
  • Remote controls and accessories
New installation

What to think through before you choose

Door Style — choose a design that complements your property's architectural style.

Material — steel, wood, aluminum, and composite each offer a different mix of appearance, durability, upkeep, and cost.

Insulation — worth considering when temperature control or energy efficiency matters, especially for attached garages.

Windows — can add natural light and visual character to the exterior.

Size & Configuration — the opening, available space, and door configuration all affect what installation actually looks like.

Beyond a single residential door

Commercial installation — commercial properties often have different requirements than residential ones: door size, usage frequency, available clearance, material, security, durability, and opener compatibility all factor in. See our commercial services page for more.

Opener installation — a new door can be paired with a compatible automatic opener, including motor installation, rail installation, wall controls, safety sensor setup, remote programming, and travel-limit setup. See our opener page for more.

Why it matters

Why professional installation matters

Garage doors are large, heavy mechanical systems containing springs, cables, tracks, and other components under significant tension. Professional installation helps ensure the system is properly aligned, securely assembled, correctly balanced, and tested before use.

Attempting to install or adjust high-tension components without the right knowledge and equipment can create serious safety risks — this isn't a place to cut corners.

Common questions

What people ask before calling.

How long does garage door installation take?

Installation time varies depending on the door type, size, existing conditions, and whether an old door needs to be removed first.

Can I replace my garage door without replacing the opener?

In some situations, an existing opener may be compatible with a new door. Compatibility and condition should be evaluated before deciding whether to keep or replace it.

Should I repair or replace my garage door?

It depends on the door's age, condition, damage, and repair history. See our replacement page for how we help you decide.

Can you install an insulated garage door?

Yes — insulated doors are available in various designs and materials. Ask about the options available for your property.

Do you install garage doors for businesses?

Commercial installation may be available depending on the project — contact us to discuss your requirements.
