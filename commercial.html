What we service

Commercial garage door problems we service

Commercial doors can develop problems from frequent use, normal wear, accidental impact, or damaged components. Common issues include:

  • Commercial garage door won't open
  • Commercial door won't close
  • Door stuck halfway
  • Damaged or bent tracks
  • Broken garage door springs
  • Broken or frayed cables
  • Worn rollers
  • Damaged hinges
  • Door alignment problems
  • Garage door opener problems
  • Noisy or rough operation
  • Damaged door panels
  • Door coming off its tracks
Our services

Commercial garage door repair services

Spring Repair — springs counterbalance the door's weight; a failed one can make a commercial door difficult or impossible to operate.

Cable Repair — damaged, frayed, or disconnected cables need professional attention to keep the lifting system working correctly.

Track Repair — bent, damaged, or misaligned tracks interfere with roller movement and cause uneven operation.

Roller Repair — worn or damaged rollers create excessive noise, vibration, and resistance.

Opener Repair — motor, drive system, control, and sensor problems can all keep a commercial door from operating correctly.

Panel Repair — impact damage affects individual panels; depending on severity, repair or replacement may be appropriate.

Why it matters

Why commercial maintenance matters

Commercial doors can be opened and closed many times a day. Frequent operation accelerates wear on rollers, cables, springs, hinges, tracks, and openers.

Regular inspection and timely repairs help catch developing problems before they become a bigger operational issue. Watch for:

  • Increasing noise
  • Slower operation
  • Uneven movement
  • Door vibration
  • Difficulty opening or closing
  • Visible component damage
  • Repeated breakdowns

When repair isn't enough

Commercial garage door replacement

Some commercial doors may be too damaged or worn for a practical repair. Replacement may be worth considering when:

  • The door has extensive structural damage
  • Major components repeatedly fail
  • Repair costs continue to increase
  • Compatible replacement parts are difficult to obtain
  • The door no longer meets the property's requirements
Common questions

What people ask before calling.

What types of commercial garage doors do you repair?

Commercial doors vary significantly by size, design, construction, and operating system. Service availability depends on the specific door and project.

How do I know if my commercial garage door needs repair?

Difficulty opening or closing, unusual noise, uneven movement, visible damage, and repeated breakdowns can all indicate the door needs inspection.

Can you repair a commercial garage door opener?

Yes — commercial opener problems can involve the motor, drive system, controls, sensors, wiring, or other components. The system should be inspected to identify the cause.

Should I repair or replace my commercial garage door?

It depends on age, condition, extent of damage, repair history, and availability of compatible parts. A professional assessment can help you compare the options.

Can a damaged commercial garage door be used?

If the door is visibly damaged, off its tracks, has a broken spring or cable, or is behaving unpredictably, continued operation may create additional safety and equipment risks. Have it inspected before continuing to use it.
