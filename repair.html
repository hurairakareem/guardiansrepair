What we fix

Common garage door problems we repair

A garage door rarely fails for just one reason. Our technicians check the whole system, springs, cables, tracks, rollers, and the opener, so the fix addresses what's actually wrong instead of just the symptom you noticed.

We offer same day garage door repair and 24 hour garage door repair availability for urgent situations, alongside standard scheduled visits for non-urgent issues. Every repair comes with clear, affordable garage door repair pricing explained before we start.

  • Door won't open or close fully
  • Door reverses immediately after closing
  • Loud grinding, popping, or scraping noises
  • Door is off its tracks or moving unevenly
  • Broken springs, cables, or rollers
  • Storm and weather-related damage
