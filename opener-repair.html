What we see

Common garage door opener problems

A malfunctioning opener can show several different symptoms, including:

  • Garage door won't open
  • Garage door won't close
  • Opener motor runs but door doesn't move
  • Remote control doesn't work
  • Wall button doesn't respond
  • Opener makes unusual noises
  • Garage door reverses while closing
  • Safety sensors aren't working correctly
  • Garage door opens but won't close
  • Opener works intermittently
  • Remote loses connection
  • Opener light isn't functioning properly
What we check

Garage door opener components we check

An opener works together with several components. Depending on the problem, an inspection may include:

Opener Motor — provides the power to move the door. Drive System — chain, belt, or screw depending on the opener. Safety Sensors — help prevent the door from closing on an obstruction.

Remote Controls — let you operate the door from outside the garage. Wall Controls — another way to open and close the door. Travel & Limit Settings — affect how far the door opens or closes. Rail & Trolley — transfers the opener's movement to the door.

Repair or replace?

Opener repair vs. replacement

Repair may be appropriate when the opener is otherwise in good condition, the problem is isolated to a replaceable component, compatible parts are available, and the system is relatively modern.

Replacement may be worth considering when the opener is significantly outdated, major components have failed, repairs are becoming frequent, parts are difficult to obtain, or the opener is incompatible with your current door.

Diagnosing the issue

Sometimes the door is the real problem

An opener isn't always the source of the problem. A door that's difficult to lift, out of balance, or dealing with spring, cable, roller, or track issues can place extra strain on the opener.

That's why diagnosing the complete garage door system matters, rather than simply replacing the opener and hoping it fixes things.

  • Opener runs but the door barely moves → often a door/spring issue
  • Opener doesn't respond at all → often electrical or remote-related
  • Door reverses immediately → often a sensor alignment issue
Safety

Garage door opener safety

Openers work with heavy moving doors and safety components. If your door is behaving unexpectedly, repeatedly reversing, becoming stuck, or making unusual sounds, avoid forcing the system to operate.

Safety sensors and other protective components should be properly installed and functioning before regular use.

Common questions

What people ask before calling.

Why won't my garage door opener work?

There can be several causes, including power problems, remote issues, safety sensors, wiring, opener components, or problems with the door itself.

Why does my garage door opener run but the door doesn't move?

The problem may involve the trolley, drive mechanism, door connection, or another component. The complete system should be inspected to find the cause.

Why does my garage door reverse when closing?

Safety sensors, obstructions, incorrect settings, track problems, or other mechanical issues can all cause a door to reverse.

Should I repair or replace my garage door opener?

It depends on the opener's age, condition, failure type, availability of replacement parts, and compatibility with your door.

Can you install a new garage door opener?

Yes — we can help you select and install a compatible system.
