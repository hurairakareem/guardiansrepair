Cable Repair

Professional garage door cable repair

Garage door cables work with the springs, drums, and other components to help the door move smoothly. When a cable becomes loose, frayed, broken, or comes off the drum, the door can become uneven, stuck, or unsafe to operate.

Signs to watch for

Signs of a garage door cable problem

You may have a cable-related problem if:

  • One side of the garage door is higher than the other
  • The door hangs unevenly
  • A cable appears loose or disconnected
  • You see fraying or visible damage
  • The garage door gets stuck
  • The door won't open or close properly
  • The cable has come off the drum
  • You hear unusual sounds when operating the door
  • The garage door suddenly becomes difficult to move
Common problems

Common garage door cable problems

Broken cable — can eventually break because of wear, corrosion, improper tension, or damage, preventing the door from operating correctly.

Frayed cable — visible fraying indicates the cable is wearing out, and continued use increases the risk of failure.

Cable off the drum — can become disconnected when the door is unbalanced or another component has a problem.

Uneven door — if one side moves differently from the other, the cables, springs, drums, tracks, or other components may need inspection.

Why it matters

Why garage door cables matter

Cables work as part of the door's lifting and balancing system, so a problem with one can affect how the entire door operates.

Because garage doors also contain high-tension springs and heavy moving components, cable repairs should be handled carefully by someone with the right knowledge and equipment.

Repair vs. replacement

What usually needs to happen

A cable that's severely worn, frayed, or broken generally needs to be replaced rather than repaired. The correct replacement depends on your garage door's configuration and cable system.

A professional inspection can determine whether the cable is the only problem, or whether other components also need attention.

Common questions

What people ask before calling.

What happens if a garage door cable breaks?

A broken cable can cause the door to become uneven, stuck, or difficult to operate. Other components may be affected depending on your system.

Can I operate my garage door with a broken cable?

It's best to avoid it. The door may be unstable, and continuing to operate it could damage additional components.

Why does my garage door cable keep coming off?

Possible causes include an unbalanced door, spring problems, damaged drums, or incorrect cable tension. The whole system should be inspected rather than just resetting the cable.

Do garage door cables need to be replaced in pairs?

It depends on your system and the condition of the cables. If one has failed, the age and condition of the other should also be evaluated.

How often should garage door cables be replaced?

There's no universal interval — lifespan depends on usage, environment, installation, and overall door condition. Regular inspection helps catch wear before failure.
