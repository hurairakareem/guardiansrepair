Track Repair

Professional garage door track repair

Garage door tracks guide the rollers as your door opens and closes. When a track becomes bent, misaligned, loose, or damaged, the door can become noisy, uneven, or difficult to operate.

Signs to watch for

Signs your garage door tracks need repair

A track problem can produce several noticeable symptoms:

  • Garage door moves unevenly
  • Door gets stuck while opening or closing
  • Garage door makes grinding or scraping sounds
  • Rollers come out of the track
  • Visible bends or dents in the track
  • Track appears loose from the wall
  • Door shakes or vibrates during movement
  • One side of the door moves differently from the other
  • Garage door becomes difficult to open manually
Common problems

Common garage door track problems

Bent or damaged tracks — can result from accidental impact, worn hardware, or other mechanical problems, interfering with roller movement.

Misaligned tracks — need to stay properly aligned for rollers to travel smoothly; misalignment causes friction, noise, uneven movement, and extra wear.

Loose track hardware — bolts, brackets, and fasteners can loosen over time, letting the track shift from its intended position.

Worn or damaged rollers — sometimes what looks like a track problem is actually the rollers; both need inspecting to find the real source.

When repair isn't enough

Garage door track replacement

Some damaged tracks can't be safely restored through adjustment or minor repair. A severely bent, cracked, or otherwise compromised track may need replacement.

Track replacement should be matched to your garage door's size, design, roller configuration, and overall system.

Why it matters

Why proper track alignment matters

Tracks work together with rollers, hinges, cables, springs, and other components. Poor alignment increases friction and places unnecessary stress on the rest of the system.

Addressing a track problem early can help prevent additional wear and more expensive repairs down the line.

Residential & commercial

Two different wear patterns

Residential — homeowners often see track problems caused by accidental vehicle impact, loose hardware, normal wear, damaged rollers, door imbalance, or improper previous adjustments.

Commercial — commercial doors can see additional wear from frequent daily operation. Service may involve inspecting vertical and horizontal tracks, rollers, brackets, fasteners, alignment, and related lifting components. Commercial availability may vary by project and location — see our commercial services page.

Common questions

What people ask before calling.

Why is my garage door rubbing against the track?

Possible causes include track misalignment, damaged rollers, loose hardware, or another mechanical problem. The system should be inspected to determine the exact cause.

Can a bent garage door track be repaired?

Minor issues can sometimes be corrected, while severely damaged tracks may need replacement. It depends on the track's condition and the overall door system.

Why is my garage door moving unevenly?

Uneven movement can be caused by track alignment, rollers, cables, springs, or other components. A complete inspection helps identify the source.

Can I adjust my garage door tracks myself?

We don't recommend it. Garage door systems contain heavy moving components and high-tension parts, so significant track repairs or adjustments should be handled by a qualified professional.

How can I prevent garage door track problems?

Regular inspection, keeping tracks free of obstructions, addressing unusual noises early, and maintaining the rollers and other components can help reduce unnecessary wear.
